  3. Лицензийн бүртгэл (имэйл хаяг): Хөгжүүлэгчийн бүртгэлээс имэйлээ устгуулахын тулд доорх имэйл рүү хүсэлт илгээнэ — хүсэлтийг хүлээн авснаас хойш 30 хоногийн дотор устгана.

Account & Data Deletion (English)

Kassiin Devter (Кассын Дэвтэр) · Developer: mulu1105@gmail.com · Last updated: July 2026

Key point: All of your shop data — products, sales, debts and customer details — is stored inside your own Google Drive. The developer only keeps your email address to verify your licence.

How to delete your account and data

  1. Shop data (products, sales, debts, customer info): open your Google Drive and delete the spreadsheet named «Кассын Дэвтэр (дата)». This permanently erases all shop data.
  2. App access: revoke the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  3. Licence record (email): to remove your email from the developer's licence records, email us — it will be deleted within 30 days of the request.

What is deleted / kept

  • Shop data — in your own Drive; deleted permanently when you delete the spreadsheet.
  • Email address — in the developer's licence system; deleted within 30 days on request. Kept only until you request deletion.
